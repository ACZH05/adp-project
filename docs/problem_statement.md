The Entertainment License Application process at the District Office still requires applicants to be physically present to obtain information, submit documents, and check on their application status. This process frequently causes congestion at the counter and takes a long time, as officers must manually verify the completeness of documents.

Additionally, there are cases where applicants fail to prepare complete documents or do not fully understand the stipulated application requirements, resulting in applications being returned or applicants having to revisit the office.

This manual procedure not only increases application processing time, but also negatively impacts the efficiency of service delivery to customers.
